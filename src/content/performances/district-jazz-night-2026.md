The District Jazz Night is a challenging and fast-paced performance environment. As the Lead Baritone, I performed four complex jazz pieces, holding the bass foundation for the full band. 

Big band jazz relies heavily on the baritone sax to bridge the gap between the rhythm section and the high brass. Achieving the rhythmic precision necessary for these pieces requires the same iterative practice and dedicated focus that I apply to my technical builds.
