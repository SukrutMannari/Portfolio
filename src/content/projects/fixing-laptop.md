## Troubleshooting Steps

1. **Diagnosis:** I started by diagnosing unresponsive keys on my laptop. By testing the inputs, I isolated the issue and identified a damaged connection ribbon cable between the keyboard and the motherboard.
2. **Sourcing:** After researching the specific laptop model and the type of ribbon connector used, I determined that replacing the entire keyboard assembly was the most effective fix, and I sourced a compatible replacement.

> [!NOTE]
> **Hardware Debug Box: Isolating the Fault**
> When the keys stopped working, the immediate fear was a dead motherboard or liquid damage. However, by plugging in an external USB keyboard (which worked perfectly) and then testing the continuity of the internal keyboard's membrane, I isolated the fault entirely to a micro-tear in the fragile ribbon cable.

3. **Disassembly & Repair:** I carefully disassembled the laptop casing. Crucially, I disconnected the internal battery first for safety, applied anti-static precautions, unseated the fragile motherboard ribbon connectors using a spudger, and successfully swapped the component.

## Key Skills Learned

Beyond just getting the computer fully functional again, this repair taught me precision hardware handling and the importance of anti-static precautions. Most importantly, it gave me the **hardware troubleshooting confidence and precision electronics handling** required to independently maintain and repair consumer electronics without fear of breaking them.
