## The Challenge

Modeling structurally sound, aerodynamically stable fixed-wing airframe concepts to understand center of gravity (CG) limits and structural stress points.

## The Process

I created detailed 3D CAD airframes in Fusion 360, mapping component placements (battery, servos, receiver) to analyze CG placement. These designs serve as digital templates for future real-world foam and carbon fiber airframe assemblies.
