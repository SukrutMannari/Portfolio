## The Challenge

Providing a simple, accessible way for classmates to practice for the New Jersey Student Learning Assessments (NJSLA) under a layout that matches the state's online testing interface.

## The Process

I leveraged Lovable to scaffold a mock testing console containing practice questions, navigation controls (next, back, flag question), and a score summary, making the assessment environment less intimidating for peers.
