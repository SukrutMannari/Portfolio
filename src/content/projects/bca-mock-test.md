## The Challenge

Helping friends practice for the Bergen County Academies (BCA) entrance exam by building a web application that simulates the testing environment, timing constraints, and question formats.

## The Process

Using the Lovable platform, I prompted and refined an interactive test app featuring sample math and essay questions, timers, score calculators, and feedback summaries to help students prep under real test conditions.
