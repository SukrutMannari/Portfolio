A browser-based 3D flight simulator built from scratch using Three.js and HTML5 Canvas.

**Key Features:**
- **Realistic Aerodynamics**: Custom physics engine calculating lift, drag, gravity, and thrust based on velocity and angle of attack.
- **Volumetric Clouds**: Procedurally generated high-density cloud layers using advanced sprite and fog rendering.
- **Detailed Aircraft Model**: A procedurally generated high-poly 3D airplane.
- **Interactive Controls**: Full pitch, roll, and thrust control.
